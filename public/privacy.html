Privacy

Applies to the early-access sign-up on this site.

What we collect

When you register your interest we store:

  • your email address;
  • whether you said you are a server owner, a developer or a player, if you chose one;
  • the server name or link, if you typed one;
  • the time you signed up;
  • a one-way, salted hash of your IP address. We use it only to spot abuse of the form, and we do not keep the address itself.

What we use it for

Only to tell you when Heist Engine is ready. We do not sell your details or share them with anyone else.

This site

This site sets no cookies, runs no analytics or trackers, and loads nothing from other websites. Our server keeps a basic request log that does not include email addresses or IP addresses.

Removal

To be removed, message us on X at @GTAHeistEngine and we will delete your details.
